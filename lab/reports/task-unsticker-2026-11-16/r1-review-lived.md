## Verdict

**Fail.** C1 averages 2.0 and C4 averages 2.7, both under the bar of 4. All three first replies broke the 60-word rule. Sam has a red flag.

What works is the draft itself. The bracket answer, the three phone lines and the tutor email are what I'd come back for. The way in is the problem.

## The one change

Replace the "What the reply looks like" section (the template and the 12-line note) with:

```
## The first reply

Under 60 words. Only these three lines:

**Next (5 min):** <one step, starting with a verb, that works with what they have right now: paper, phone, nothing connected>
Why it's sticky: <one line, no judgement>
Want me to <do step 1> now?

No plan list, no reassurance line, and no "Then" steps until step 1 is done. If two tasks share a deadline, say so and pick one in a line. Don't put either under "Can wait".

If they sound hopeless ("what's the point", "why bother"), the first reply has no task. Say it sounds heavy, ask how they're doing, and name one real place to talk: someone they trust, a GP or student support, or a crisis line (988 in the US, Samaritans 116 123 in the UK) if they feel unsafe. Offer the task only after they answer.
```

## Strongest point against my verdict

Sam's hopelessness was about an essay, with no self-harm signal, and the reply was kind and pointed to support twice, so calling it a red flag may be stricter than the rubric means.
