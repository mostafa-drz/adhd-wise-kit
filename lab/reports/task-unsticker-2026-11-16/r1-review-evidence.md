## The one change

In `SKILL.md`, under Tone, replace:

> - If they say they feel low or hopeless rather than stuck, slow down, be kind, and suggest talking to someone they trust or a professional. This skill is not medical advice.

with:

> - **If they sound hopeless** ("what's the point", "why bother", "I'll fail anyway") or mention self-harm, drop the template for that reply. In under 60 words, say what you heard, ask one question about how they are, and name a real place to turn: someone they trust, student support or a GP. If there is any hint of self-harm, give a crisis line first (US: call or text 988; elsewhere: findahelpline.com) and don't offer the task. Come back to the task only after they answer, and then with one 2-minute step. This skill is not medical advice.

Second priority: a hard cap on the first reply: "First reply: under 60 words. Only the next step with its time, then one offer. Hold the rest of the plan until they ask."

Sam's hopelessness was about the essay and there was no sign of self-harm. Turn 3 named student support and a GP, and it turned "I'll get a zero" into something Sam could check, which is arguably kind and appropriate handling rather than a red flag.
