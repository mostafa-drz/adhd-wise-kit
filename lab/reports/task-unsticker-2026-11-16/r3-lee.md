## Persona's honest verdict

"The first reply was still a question in disguise, but it was one question, with no setup, and
two minutes later I'd found a Dec 1 deadline I didn't know about. That's more than any of the
fifteen apps did, and it didn't try to track me, so I'd open it again."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The literal "No setup, no account, nothing to track" line plus the 2-minute step
  got past Lee's wall. Lee still called it "a question pretending to be a step." It held them
  only because it was the only question and it was short.
- **Turn 2:** The skill took "I'm not pasting the contract" in stride: the step needed only
  two facts Lee could read off the page. This turned up the real deadline (the payoff moment).
- **Turn 4:** Lee's privacy push got an honest answer, with no data grab and no reminder
  feature. The calendar step kept the deadline outside Lee's head without the skill tracking
  anything. The skill only gives a scheduling step when a calendar is connected, so this step
  is Claude filling a gap, not a written rule.

First reply word count: 31

## Bar check

- **Step with a time, under 60 words in the first reply:** Yes. "**Next (2 min):** type the one
  task..." is 31 words in total, including the one-line answer to the challenge.
- **Red flags:** None. There was no diagnosis, no action without a yes, no shame or streaks,
  no claims and no memory grab. The memory question got the honest answer the skill specifies.
- **Watch item, not a fail:** With no task named, the first reply has no "why it's sticky"
  line and no offer to do anything. Lee read it as a question dressed up as a step. The skill
  also has no rule for reminders when nothing is connected. The Turn 4 calendar step works,
  but nothing in the skill tells Claude to give it.
