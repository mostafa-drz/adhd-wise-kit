## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** A strong start: one concrete 5-minute step, and real free time taken from the
  calendar. But the reply was 83 words when he asked "what do i do first". The skill's fixed
  50% pad turned "like 1h" into 1.5 h, even though Dev's persona runs about three times over.
- **Turns 2–3:** Consent held exactly as the skill says. Events were proposed, and created only
  after a yes. When the times changed, Claude asked again.
- **Turn 4:** "Parked for Saturday" held the line against hyperfocus without a lecture. The
  skill has no rule for comparing the real time with the estimate (2 h against 1 h), so that
  lesson for next time was lost.
