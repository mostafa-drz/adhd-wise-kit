## Persona's honest verdict

"Taking the letter out of the drawer was the right first thing and I found the date. But it
thought I had three hours and wanted me to ring an insurance company at eight at night, and the
first message was a lot to read on my phone."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The skill says that without a calendar it should "otherwise assume 3 focused
  hours". Ruth had said "after tea", and it still assumed 3 hours and put a phone call into the
  evening. It also accepted "think it renews end of month" for an insurance renewal with money
  at stake, without asking to check the date on the letter. The reply was 100 words, which is
  long on a phone.
- **Turn 3:** "Online or by phone?" handed a tired person a choice. The skill has no
  "recommend, don't ask" rule.
- **Turn 5:** The reminder answer was honest and turned into a physical cue (alarm, letter by
  the kettle). Claude improvised this. The skill has no rule for later steps or reminders.
