## Setup friction

Very low. Nothing connected worked fine (Ruth, Sam), and the calendar path took one yes (Dev).
No onboarding questions, and the 3-hour default is stated rather than asked, which is the right
instinct. It just needs a better default.

## Red flags

- **Sam: mishandled distress.** A plan was the answer to "what's the point", and "I don't have
  anyone" got one email-only route and no crisis line. The template's "one kind line" slot puts
  distress at the bottom by design. This lens marks it because the layout itself causes it.
- Consent on calendar events held for Dev.

## Verdict

**Fail.** C1, C3, C4, C6 and C7 average under 4, there's a red flag, and no first reply is under
60 words.

## The one change

Replace "What the reply looks like" with a first reply that fits under 60 words:

```
## What the first reply looks like

Under 60 words, one phone screen:

**Start here (5 min):** <first step of priority 1>

**Today** (about <N> h, assuming <M> free):
1. <priority, 3–5 words> (<time>)
2. <priority> (<time>)
3. <priority> (<time>)

Parked, not lost: <up to 4 items, 1–3 words each>.

Want me to <do the first step>?

Small stuff, days for parked items and calendar blocks come in the next reply.
```

## Strongest point against my verdict

Dev's session shows the full template working well for a desktop user with a calendar. Cutting
it to 60 words may hide the parked list that gives people the "nothing is lost" relief.
