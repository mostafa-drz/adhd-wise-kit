## Persona's honest verdict

"It opened Dana's email for me and the first thing I saw was 'no panic', which is exactly what I needed, and it never put words in my mouth this time. I only half-noticed it didn't flag the enrollment deadline until after Dana, and that would have scared me if I'd run out of steam first."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The named-message path did the scary first look and led with the relief, in 52 words with one timed step. This is the fix round 1 asked for. The cost: the benefits deadline tomorrow stayed invisible until the first item was done. The skill puts "named message first" ahead of "nearest real consequence" and has no line for a hard deadline within 24 hours.
- **Turns 2-3:** The draft left a visible blank for the missing date instead of inventing "Wednesday", and nothing was saved to Gmail. Two small things: "Happy to" adds a feeling Maya never stated (the skill bans that, but Claude slips it in by habit), and Claude sent the edited text without showing it first. Maya's edits were exact, so it worked, but the skill doesn't say whether a "yes, after these changes" needs a re-show.
- **Turn 5:** Claude didn't guess what Sam's unopened invoice says or whether it can wait. It gave a plain if-then cue for tomorrow. With Calendar connected, the skill gives no guidance on offering to block the time, so Claude offered nothing.
