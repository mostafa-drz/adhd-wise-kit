## Persona's honest verdict

"It picked the right two out of twelve on the first try, caught a phishing email, and didn't ask
me one onboarding question, which is more than most of these tools manage. But it offered me
Gmail after I'd said Outlook and no connecting, the first reply was just 'paste stuff', and it
kept saying 'send it' when it can't send anything."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The opening line from step 2, used word for word, offers Gmail to someone who said
  Outlook and "don't ask". It also has no step and no time. Lee nearly left before seeing
  any value.
- **Turn 2:** Sorting by consequence, not age, plus the phishing flag, is where Lee changed
  his mind. A timed step (the 2-minute payroll action) shows up for the first time here, not
  in the first reply.
- **Turn 3:** The draft left the avoided part, the status, as "[status]", and "Send it, change
  it, or next?" made no sense without a connector. The changed draft also added a promise Lee
  hadn't made ("I'm chasing them").
