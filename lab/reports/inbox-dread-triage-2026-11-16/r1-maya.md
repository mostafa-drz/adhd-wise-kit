- **Persona:** `lab/personas/maya.md` (34, inattentive ADHD plus anxiety, marketing manager)
- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-kit/skills/inbox-dread-triage/SKILL.md`
- **Setup:** claude.ai on laptop, Gmail and Calendar connected. Monday 16 Nov 2026, 6:20pm.
- **Invented inbox (fictional):** 41 unread. Among them: manager Dana Whitfield, "Q4 paid social, quick question" (Thu 12 Nov, unopened, 4 days); People Ops, "Open enrollment closes Tue Nov 18" (real deadline, form); client Jordan Pike (Brightline), "Re: Holiday assets, approval?" ## Persona's honest verdict

"It looked at Dana's email so I didn't have to, and 'no panic' undid four days of dread in one line. But the first screen was a lot, it found a draft in my Gmail I hadn't asked for, and it nearly had me promise Jordan something that wasn't true."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The skill's sort put the benefits form ahead of the email Maya named as the scary one. Dana's email came second, and the relief was buried inside a draft at the bottom of a 128-word reply. The layout has no place for "here's what the scary one says, and it's small", which is the thing Maya came for.
- **Turns 1-2 and 3:** The skill tells Claude to save drafts in Gmail without asking. For an anxious user, an unexpected draft read like a sent email for a moment. Writing to the mailbox without a yes is not on the list of banned actions, but here it felt like one.
- **Turn 3:** Claude moved straight on to the next draft while Maya was off doing enrollment, and the Jordan draft claimed approvals nobody had checked. The skill says to write in her voice but never says to leave out facts Claude can't verify.
