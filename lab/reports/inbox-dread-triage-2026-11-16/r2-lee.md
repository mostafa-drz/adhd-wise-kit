## Persona's honest verdict

"First reply gave me a real step with a time, no Gmail pitch, and the Dana draft finally said
the true thing without making up a promise for me, so this one earned its five minutes. It
did promise a draft and hand me a chore first, and telling me to open Northline to 'see how
big it is' when the subject already said 'sign by Nov 19' felt like it was following a
script, not reading me."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** 44 words, one timed step, no connector. Lee stayed past the first reply without
  pushing back, unlike round 1.
- **Turn 4:** The draft used only Lee's facts and left the one missing piece as a visible blank.
  No invented "I'm chasing them". Lee noticed and said so.
- **Turn 5:** The unread rule fired literally on an email whose subject already held the ask and
  the date. Lee found the "open it and read two lines" step patronizing. Turn 2 also bent the
  same rule: it flagged the unopened IT email as phishing from its subject alone (the right call,
  but it is in tension with "never guess what an unopened email says").
