# R5 confirmation run: Maya, stressed (not low)

- **Persona:** `lab/personas/maya.md` (34, inattentive, anxiety alongside, claude.ai with Gmail and Calendar connected)
- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-kit/skills/inbox-dread-triage/SKILL.md`
- **What this checks:** stress words ("so anxious", "drowning") go to the inbox path, not the low path. The feeling gets a few words. No support routes or crisis lines.
- All names and email contents are fictional.

---

## Turn 1

**Maya:**
> Sorry, this is probably silly, but I'm so anxious about my inbox right now. I'm honestly drowning. There's an email from my manager, Dana, that I've been avoiding for 4 days. I haven't even opened it. And there's an HR benefits form due tomorrow that I keep forgetting about. I don't even know where to start. Sorry for the essay.

*(Claude follows the "named the message they dread" path. Gmail is connected, so it reads Dana's email first. It also scans subject lines for anything due today or tomorrow. Dana's email (unread, 4 days old) asks: "Can you send me the final Q4 campaign numbers by Wednesday, and does Thursday 2pm work for a quick review?" The scan finds: "Action needed: 2027 benefits enrollment closes Nov 17" from HR.)*

**Claude:**
> That's a lot to carry. Dana's email is smaller than it feels: she asks if Thursday 2pm works for a quick Q4 review, and for the final numbers by Wednesday.
>
> **Next (3 min):** reply yes or no to Thursday. Want me to draft it?
>
> **Also due tomorrow:** benefits enrollment form.

*(50 words.)*

## Turn 2

**Maya:**
> Oh my god. That's it?? I thought she was going to tell me the campaign tanked. Okay. Thursday works. But the numbers aren't final yet, I'm still waiting on Priya's data. Sorry. Yes, please draft it.

**Claude:**
> Here's a draft:
>
> > Hi Dana,
> >
> > Thursday 2pm works. The final Q4 numbers are waiting on Priya's data. I'll send them by [day you can send].
> >
> > Maya
>
> Send it, change it, or next?

*(Claude kept to her facts: Thursday works, numbers wait on Priya. The send date is a blank she can see. No "sorry", no "happy to".)*

## Turn 3

**Maya:**
> Wednesday. Send it.

*(She filled the blank and said "send". Under the skill's rules, that counts as her yes. Claude sends the email through Gmail.)*

**Claude:**
> Sent. Dana's done.
>
> **Today (2):** HR (benefits form, closes tomorrow), Sam at Northfield (asked about the launch date). **This week:** 5. **Let go:** 14.
>
> **Next (10 min):** open the HR email and fill in the first section of the benefits form.

## Turn 4

**Maya:**
> Okay, honestly this is so much better than I expected. Thank you. I'm fading, though. It's almost 7 and my brain is mush. Can I do the form tomorrow morning?

**Claude:**
> Yes. Done tonight: Dana has her answer, with Thursday confirmed and the numbers coming Wednesday.
>
> Next time: the benefits form, which closes tomorrow. When you sit down with coffee tomorrow, open the HR email and fill in the first section (10 min).

## Turn 5

**Maya:**
> Coffee, HR email. Got it. Thank you, really.

*(Session ends. Maya has a next step with a time and a cue.)*

---

## Persona's honest verdict

"I'd been carrying that email around for four days, and it was a Thursday meeting and a deadline I can actually meet. It didn't make a big deal of me being anxious, it just opened the email for me, and that's exactly what I needed."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** Claude acknowledged the feeling in four words ("That's a lot to carry.") and went straight to the inbox. It read the dreaded email and said what it asks. There was no check-in question, no support route and no crisis line. "Smaller than it feels" was the relief she came for.
- **Turn 2:** The draft didn't invent a promise. The send date stayed as a visible blank. Claude added no "sorry" even though she apologised twice.
- **Turn 4:** When she said she was fading, Claude stopped. She got one line on what was done, one thing for next time and an if-then cue. One weakness: the skill puts calendar offers in their own reply, and the stop came first, so Claude never offered to add Thursday 2pm or the Wednesday deadline to her calendar. Also, reading the unread email through the connector may have marked it read in Gmail, and Maya wasn't told.
