## Persona's honest verdict

"It took the scary first look for me, and this time it told me about enrollment straight away without making it sound like an emergency, which honestly saved me. Showing me Dana's email again after my edit felt safe, but showing Jordan's again when all I did was add 'Wednesday noon' and say send was one step too many at 7pm."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** 57 words, one timed step (3 min), and the "Also due tomorrow" line put the enrollment deadline in front of Maya from the subject line alone, without guessing what the unopened email said. This was the main gap in round 2 and it's fixed.
- **Turns 3 and 6:** The edits rule ran literally both times. For Dana (a wording change, "a quick summary") the re-show was worth it and Maya was glad to see it. For Jordan she only filled the blank the skill had left, and had already said "Send", so re-showing it added a turn and made her snap. The skill doesn't treat filling a blank differently from changing the wording.
- **Turn 2:** The draft had no "happy to" and no invented date or format (round 2 slipped in "write-up" and "Happy to"). "Thanks for flagging this" is the only phrase Maya didn't supply, and it's standard courtesy, not a promise. Still, Calendar is connected and the skill says nothing about blocking time for Wednesday's summary or for Sam tomorrow, so Claude offered nothing.
