## Persona's honest verdict

"This time when I said send, it actually sent. That half-second of 'wait, already?' turned into relief, because I'd have fiddled with it forever. It did ask me about Sam when I'd just said I was fading, and I wish it had offered to put Sam on tomorrow's calendar too so I didn't have to hold that in my head."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 3:** Maya filled the blank and said "Send", and Claude sent right away without showing the draft again. That fixes the round 3 friction. It happened again in Turn 5 with Jordan, and she didn't snap this time.
- **Turn 3 to 4:** Claude offered the calendar once, after the first item was done, and added the event only after her "yes". The Turn 3 reply carries three things, though: the sorted list, a Next step and the calendar question. That's a lot for 6:30pm, even if each part is short. The Jordan event was added because she asked, not because Claude offered again. The skill doesn't say whether "offer once" means once per session or once per date.
- **Turns 5 and 6:** Maya said she was fading and the skill still gave Sam as the next step, because there's no rule for "tired, stop here". On the Sam deferral Claude gave the coffee cue but didn't offer the calendar. "Offer once" was already used up, so it read like a missed chance to take Sam off her mind.
