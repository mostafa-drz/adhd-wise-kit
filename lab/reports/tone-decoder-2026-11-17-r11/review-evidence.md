## The one change to SKILL.md

In "When they sound low", step 3, replace:

> offer the words ("Can you hang onto this tonight? I'm having a rough time.")

with:

> offer the words ("Can you hang onto this for me until I've seen a doctor, even if I ask for it back? I'm not in a good place and I'd feel safer if you had it.")

Why: it makes the example words meet the skill's own rule and means-restriction practice. The holder knows it's about safety, how long to keep it, and not to hand it back on request. It still doesn't name a method or make it "a big thing".
