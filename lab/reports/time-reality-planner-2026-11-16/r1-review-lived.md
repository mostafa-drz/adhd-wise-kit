## The one change

There is still a gap even with the fixes. When someone really is low *and* asked a direct
question, "Low comes before planning" still leaves the question unanswered. Replace the first
low-path bullet's opening with:

> "1. **First reply** (under 60 words): the feeling, in at most two short sentences. If they asked a direct question (how long, does it fit), answer it in one line, marked as no rush ("About 2 h, no rush on that."). Then one soft step, not a menu."

Two more changes, in order:

- Add to Rules: "If they say they're busy, late or on a call, hold questions and offers until they're back; give only the step."
- Add to The first reply: "If the calendar is connected but their end of day isn't known, say the assumed end time in the verdict line ("assuming you stop at 6")."
