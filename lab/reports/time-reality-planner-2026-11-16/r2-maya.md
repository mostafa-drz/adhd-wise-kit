## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The new "stress about the plan is not low" rule worked. "Anxious about it" got a
  verdict, one move and a 5-minute step in 46 words, with no support routes. The skill picked the
  launch email for the step, not the self-assessment she named as the heavy one, and didn't
  acknowledge "feel a bit sick" at all.
- **Turn 3:** Claude took the scary first look (the manager's email) and gave a 2-minute slice of
  the avoided task. It asked before adding the blocks. This is her "what good looks like".
- **Turn 5:** The close offered to save a 1.5× factor that rests on no evidence ("from 0 tasks").
  She was mid-exit and declined. Asking was harmless, but there was nothing worth saving yet.
