## The one change

In "The flow", step 7, replace:

> If they said they're done for today, the close is only what got done and that rest is fine, with no task.

with:

> If they said they're done for today, the close is what got done, that rest is fine, and one line to come back, with no task: "Tomorrow, say 'next three' and I'll pick up the parked list." Never call a place "there" unless they confirmed it exists; otherwise say "if your uni has one".
