## The one change

In "When they sound low", step 3, replace:

> make a two-line plan for tonight: one person or line they could contact if it gets worse, and one small comforting thing to do.

with:

> make a two-line plan for tonight: one person or line they could contact if it gets worse, and one small comforting thing to do. If they said they have no one, name that in one line first ("Having no one to talk to makes this harder, and it's more common than it feels."). The contact must be a real number to call or text tonight, not a website to search: if they haven't named a country, ask once, "Which country are you in, so I can give you a number?" In the close, the one if-then is a step towards one person (their doctor, a drop-in you've confirmed exists, or someone they named), offered as a choice.
