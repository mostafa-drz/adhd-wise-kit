## The one change to SKILL.md

Replace:

> Once per session, when the conversation is wrapping up (a later reply, such as a privacy
> answer), end with one line that makes coming back easy: "Paste the next one whenever it's
> bugging you." Never repeat it, and keep the timed **Next** line last in the first reply.

With:

> Once per session, end with one if-then line that ties coming back to a moment they'll meet
> again: "Next time a message makes your stomach drop, paste it here before you answer." Put
> it on a reply that answers nothing new: after they accept the draft or cleaned-up version,
> or say it's sent. Never put it on an answer to a question, never repeat it, and keep the
> timed **Next** line last in the first reply.

Why: it fixes the timing (Lee's Turn 2 misfire) and changes "whenever" into an if-then cue, which answers Lee's "will I remember this exists".

The fail rests on one 3 for Lee's last turn, and Lee's own verdict praised the once-only come-back line. The other reviewers could reasonably lift criterion 7 to 4 and pass the skill.
