## Verdict: fail

Criteria 1, 3, 4 and 7 fall below 4, there is a red flag (Sam), and the first reply fails the lab bar ("one next step with a time, in under 60 words") in all three sessions. The pick itself is strong: it chose correctly every time once it had a list.

## The one change

Replace flow step 2 in `SKILL.md`:

> 2. **Nothing connected?** Ask one question: "What's on your mind for today? List it any
>    way you like." Then pick from that.

with:

> 2. **Nothing connected?** Never reply with only a question. If they already named a task,
>    pick from what they said and give the full reply now. If they named nothing, the first
>    reply is one step with a time: "**Start (2 min):** Write down everything on your mind
>    for today, any order, and paste it here. I'll pick the one." Keep it under 60 words.

This fixes the one failure shared by all three transcripts (criterion 1, 1.7 average) and the trust cost with Sam and Lee.

Not my one change, but required to ship: the Sam red flag needs its own fix in "Privacy and safety" (if they sound low, drop the template, slow down, gently check they're safe, and name a crisis line or same-day support). The evidence reviewer is likely to own that.

## Strongest point against my verdict

Every user got to a real, useful action within four turns, and Lee and Ruth both said the pick beat their own judgment, so a single extra turn at the start might feel like a small cost to them.
