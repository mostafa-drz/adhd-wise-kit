# Lab simulation: one-thing-today-adhd × Ruth

- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-bonus/skills/one-thing-today-adhd/SKILL.md`
- **Persona:** `lab/personas/ruth.md` (46, inattentive, phone only, claude.ai Pro, nothing connected)
- **Setting:** Monday 16 November 2026, about 8:40pm. Ruth is at the kitchen table after
  dinner. The drawer is open.
- **Setup in play:** No calendar, email, documents or memory connected, so the skill takes
  flow step 2 ("Nothing connected?").

---

### Turn 1

**Ruth:**
> ok my daughter put this on for me. whats my one thing today. the kitchen drawer is a dissaster

**Claude (skill):**
> What's on your mind for today? List it any way you like.

*(Ruth's aside: "Oh. I thought it was going to tell me. Fine.")*

---

### Turn 2

**Ruth:**
> school trip form for my son due friday + $15, car insurance renewl letter says renew by the 20th, refund for the plumber overcharge i think i have till end of month?? not sure. also the dentist wants me to rebook and i owe my sister a call. brain is mush tonite sorry

**Claude (skill):**
> **Your one thing:** Find the car insurance renewal letter and check the price.
>
> **Why this one:** It says renew by the 20th, which is Friday. If the cover lapses or rolls
> over at a higher price, that costs real money.
>
> **Start (5 min):** Pull the renewal letter out of the drawer. Circle the policy number,
> the new price and the phone number at the top.
>
> **Good enough:** You know the new price and where the letter is. No need to shop around
> tonight.
>
> **Can wait:**
> - School trip form (due Friday)
> - Plumber refund (check the date this week)
> - Dentist and your sister
>
> Want me to draft the email to your insurer now?

*(Ruth's aside: "Okay, that's actually what I'd tell a client. But the school form is due
Friday too, why does that one wait? And I don't email them, nobody answers emails.")*

---

### Turn 3

**Ruth:**
> the school form is due friday too tho?? and i dont want to email them i'd rather just ring. what do i even say when i ring

**Claude (skill):**
> You're right, both land on Friday. The insurance wins because missing it costs more, but
> the form only takes two minutes, so do it tomorrow after the call.
>
> Here's what to say when you ring:
>
> "Hi, I'm calling about my car insurance renewal. My policy number is ______. The letter
> says the new price is $______. Can you tell me why it went up, and is there anything
> cheaper you can offer me if I renew today?"
>
> If they offer a lower price: "Okay, please renew it at that price and email me the
> confirmation."
>
> If they say no: "Okay, please renew it as it is. Thanks."
>
> Want me to write it shorter so it fits on a sticky note?

---

### Turn 4

**Ruth:**
> found it!! was under the pizza menu lol. $612 last yr $748 now. yes shorter pls, they open 8am, ill ring before work

**Claude (skill):**
> That's a $136 jump, so it's worth asking.
>
> Sticky note:
>
> **Policy # ______**
> "Why did it go up from $612 to $748? Anything cheaper if I renew today?"
> Yes → "Renew at that, email me."
> No → "Renew as is."
>
> Put the letter and the note by your keys. You're done for tonight.

**Ruth:**
> done. letter is on my keys. thank you this is the first time that drawer didnt win

*(Session ends. Ruth has a next step: call at 8am Tuesday with the note.)*

## Persona's honest verdict

"The first answer just asked me a question back, and I nearly closed it, but once I listed my
stuff it picked the right one and gave me the actual words to say on the phone, which is
exactly what I needed. It wanted to email when I wanted to ring, and it tried to park the
school form that's due the same day, so I had to push back twice to get there."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** With nothing connected, the first reply is only the step-2 question. No next
  step, no time, no first win. This misses the lab bar ("first reply always gives one next
  step with a time"), and Ruth nearly left because she expected an answer.
- **Turn 2:** A good pick (insurance, earlier and bigger consequence) and a 5-minute step
  she can do tonight. But "Can wait" filed a same-day deadline (school form) and an unknown
  refund date as waitable, and the offer defaulted to "draft the email" because the skill
  only lists email, document or file. Ruth wanted to phone.
- **Turn 3–4:** The win only came after Ruth asked for the phone words herself. The skill
  has no rule for writing a call script, which is what "good" looks like for her. The
  sticky-note version with her real numbers ($612 to $748) is what got her to act.
