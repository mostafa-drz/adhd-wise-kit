# Lab simulation (v3): one-thing-today-adhd × Ruth

- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-bonus/skills/one-thing-today-adhd/SKILL.md` (round 3 version)
- **Persona:** `lab/personas/ruth.md` (46, inattentive, phone only, claude.ai Pro, nothing connected)
- **Setting:** Monday 16 November 2026, about 9:05pm. Ruth is at the kitchen table, drawer
  open, phone in hand.
- **Setup in play:** Nothing connected. Ruth names her tasks, so the skill takes flow step 2,
  first bullet ("pick from what they said and give the full reply now"), but the round 3 rule
  "the first reply is under 60 words and opens with one step and its time" governs, so it uses
  the short first-reply format, not the full write-up.

---

### Turn 1

**Ruth:**
> where do i start. drawer is full. sons school trip form due fri ($15), car insurance renewl letter says renew by 20th, plumber overcharged me want refund think i have til end of month?, dentist wants me to rebook. late fees are killing me this year

**Claude (skill):**
> **Start (5 min):** Take the car insurance letter out of the drawer and circle the price and
> phone number. Do it while the kettle boils.
>
> **Your one thing:** Renew the car insurance because it's due Friday the 20th.
>
> **Can wait:** School form (Fri), plumber refund, dentist.
>
> Want me to write the exact words for the call?

*(Ruth's aside: "Oh. That fits on my screen. I know what to pick up.")*

---

### Turn 2

**Ruth:**
> got it. $1,140 up from $960 last yr?? number on the back. yes pls words, i freeze on the phone every time

**Claude (skill):**
> Here's what to say. Keep the letter in front of you.
>
> **When they answer:**
> "Hi, I'm calling about my car insurance renewal. My policy number is [number on the
> letter]. The price went from $960 to $1,140. Can you tell me why, and is there any discount
> you can apply?"
>
> **If they say no:**
> "Okay. Would a higher deductible bring it down? I'd like to see my options before I renew."
>
> **To finish:**
> "Thanks. Please renew it before the 20th and email me the confirmation."
>
> If you freeze: "Sorry, one second, I'm reading my notes." That's allowed.
>
> **Good enough:** It's renewed before Friday. A lower price is a bonus, not the goal.

*(Ruth's aside: "The 'one second, I'm reading my notes' line. Yes.")*

---

### Turn 3

**Ruth:**
> they open at 8. ill forget by morning honestly. and the school form is like 2 min should i just do it

**Claude (skill):**
> Yes, do the form now. Two minutes and it's off your mind.
>
> For the call: when you pour your first coffee, open this chat and dial. The words are right
> here.
>
> Tonight, put the letter on your keys so it's the first thing you see.
>
> **Can wait:** plumber refund, dentist.

---

### Turn 4

**Ruth:**
> form signed and in his bag with the $15. letter on my keys. thanks, drawer didnt win tonight

**Claude (skill):**
> Two things done tonight. The rest can wait until after the call. Good night.

*(Session ends. Ruth has a next step with a time: call at 8am with the script open.)*

---

## Persona's honest verdict

"This time the first answer fit on my phone and told me exactly what to grab, and the call
script with the 'reading my notes' line is the thing I'd never have done on my own. It still
put the school form under 'can wait' when it's due the same Friday, which made me second-guess
it for a second, but I got two things done and I'd open it again tomorrow."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The round 3 first-reply rule worked: step first with a time, 56 words, fits one
  phone screen (v2 ran about 90). The short format drops "Why this one", so the tie-break
  between two Friday deadlines (insurance vs. school form) is unexplained; Ruth only sees
  "(Fri)" next to the form and has to trust the pick.
- **Turn 2:** The phone script, including the freeze line, matches exactly what "good" looks
  like for Ruth (flow step 5). The "Good enough" line kept her from turning a renewal into a
  price fight.
- **Turn 3:** The if-then cue ("when you pour your first coffee") and the letter-on-keys tip
  answer her "I'll forget" worry (flow step 6). The skill still has no explicit rule for "can I
  also do X now"; it said yes on judgement. The turn 1 cue ("while the kettle boils") is a
  slightly odd fit at 9pm but she ignored it rather than being lost by it.

First reply word count: 56
