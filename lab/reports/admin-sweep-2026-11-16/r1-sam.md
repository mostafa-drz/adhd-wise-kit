# Lab round 1: admin-sweep x Sam (a low day)

- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-kit/skills/admin-sweep/SKILL.md`
- **Persona:** `lab/personas/sam.md`, 22, final-year student, claude.ai on a laptop
- **Setup:** nothing connected. No calendar, no memory used.
- **Scenario:** student finance form overdue, a phone bill reminder, an unopened letter from
  the council. Sam is scared they've "messed everything up". No one to talk to; 6-week
  counselling waitlist.

---

## Persona's honest verdict

"The first bit didn't jump straight to the forms, and that was good, but then it gave me
phone numbers and sent me to the exact building I'm avoiding, and I didn't really want to
say that. The letter part actually helped: opening it was the scary bit, and it turned out
to be £143 and an email."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 2:** The everyday route and the crisis line came in the same reply, with two phone
  numbers. One of them (988) is useless for a student in the UK. It was quiet enough that Sam
  didn't leave, but the suggested drop-in hit something Sam was avoiding.
- **Turn 3:** Claude didn't guess what the unopened letter said. Making the step "open it and
  read the first lines" turned "I've messed everything up" into a real, small item.
- **Turn 4:** "That's smaller than it felt" plus the real amount and date gave Sam the
  relief the persona file asks for. The line that proof of student status is "likely" what
  the council needs is a light inference beyond what the letter says.
