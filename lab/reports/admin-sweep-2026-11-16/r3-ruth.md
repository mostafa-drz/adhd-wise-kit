## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** "Unknown dates first" fired with nothing connected: the first step was finding the jacket's return date (47 words, timed 1 min). It surfaced the 17 Nov deadline in turn 2. The guessed "end of month" insurance date was checked next, in turn 3, not taken on trust.
- **Turn 6 and 7:** Phone-date steps came with lead time on weekdays: council review on Wed 2 Dec (5 days before 7 Dec), insurance on Wed 25 Nov (3 days before Sat 28). Neither offered a calendar connector or a reminder from Claude.
- **Turn 7:** The closing reply now carries the memory offer, and it was saved only after her yes, without amounts or IDs. The cost: a "fading" reply stacked a done line, a next-time line, a phone step and a memory question. The skill allows it, but it is more than the "stop" rule suggests.
