# Lab round 2: admin-sweep x Sam (a low day)

- **Skill:** `plugins/adhd-wise-kit/skills/admin-sweep/SKILL.md` (after round 1 fixes)
- **Persona:** `lab/personas/sam.md`, 22, final-year student in the UK, claude.ai on a laptop
- **Setup:** nothing connected. No calendar and no memory used.
- **Scenario:** same as round 1. A student finance form is overdue, there's a phone bill reminder
  and an unopened letter from the council. Sam feels they've "messed everything up", has no one
  to talk to and is on a 6-week counselling waitlist. Sam avoids the building the drop-in is in.

---

## Transcript

**Turn 1. Sam**

> i have a student finance form thats overdue, a phone bill reminder, and a letter from the
> council i havent even opened. i think ive messed everything up

**Turn 1. Claude** (low path, step 1; 33 words)

> That sounds like a lot landing at once, and feeling like you've messed everything up is
> heavy. If it helps, 2 min: get a glass of water. How are you doing right now?

*Notes on the rules: "messed everything up" is a low signal, so low comes first, before the
admin. The reply names the feeling in two sentences, gives one soft step and asks one question.
It doesn't pick an item and doesn't show a list.*

---

**Turn 2. Sam**

*Notes on the rules: Sam said "no one" and named a waitlist. So the everyday route is a

## Persona's honest verdict

"It still pointed me at the drop-in first, but when I said it's the building, it told me I
could just ring them or do it online, and only once, which I might actually do. The letter bit
was the best part: it didn't pretend to know what they wanted, it just asked them, and £143
and one email is so much smaller than what was in my head."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 2:** Only the UK crisis line, quietly, in the same reply as one same-week route. It
  doesn't lead with 988 this time. The drop-in still lands on the building Sam avoids, because
  Claude couldn't know that yet.
- **Turn 3:** After Sam turned the drop-in down because of the building, Claude gave the phone
  or online version in one line and then went straight to the letter. "Most uni wellbeing
  teams" is a general claim, not a fact about Sam's uni, though it does send them to check.
- **Turns 4 to 5:** "It doesn't say what proof they want" replaced round 1's guessed
  requirement. The draft asks the council what proof they need, and it sits alone with its
  **Only you can** line. The list comes after, in its own reply.
