## Persona's honest verdict

"It skipped the pitch and just fixed the message, which is the only convincing I respect. But
the first rewrite changed what I meant, and nothing told me what to do next or why I'd come
back."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** Check mode ran by the book: how it lands, three fixes, a cleaned-up version. But
  the rewrite softened the *substance*: a firm deadline became a question ("if Thursday
  works") and a true fact was cut. The skill says "keep their voice" but says nothing about
  keeping their meaning. The reply was 81 words, with no next step or time; the person had to
  work out the next move.
- **Turn 1:** Showing three fixes *and* the full cleaned-up version meant reading the same
  message twice in two forms.
- **Turn 3:** The privacy answer was improvised. The skill has no rule for "is this saved?",
  so a different run could easily say something wrong or vague.
