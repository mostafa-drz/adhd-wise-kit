## Verdict

**Fail.** C1, C3, C4, C6 and C7 average under 4. No first reply has a timed next step or comes in
under 60 words. Sam has a red flag.

What I'd come back for: "I read it" for Maya. Having someone open the scary email and say it's
small is the whole product. And the 15-word reply drafts were in the sender's register.

## The one change

Replace the Decode "What it looks like" block with:

```
What the first reply looks like (under 60 words):

**Most likely:** <one sentence>

Why: "<quote>": <what it signals>. "<quote>": <what it signals>.

Less likely: <one line, only if the words support it>

**Next (2 min):** <the reply move, e.g. "send a two-line yes">. Want me to draft it?
```
