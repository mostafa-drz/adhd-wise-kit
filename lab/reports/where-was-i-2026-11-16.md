## What the lab found

- **Estimates promised to others.** Dev's raw 10h guess went to a client. Now a draft that
  promises a time carries one line under it: "People often run over; 10h might be 15."
- **Empty structure.** "Here's the short version" introduced nothing for Lee (nothing
  connected). Lines with nothing real behind them are now left out.
- **Restart, don't search.** Once they've said what they were doing, the step is doing its
  first slice. One exception: a look of 3 minutes or less when they don't know who has the
  ball.
- **Slumps mentioned in passing** get one plain line, no pep talk ("Slumps happen. Let's
  find one thread."). "You're doing it" read as a poster.
- **The low path's first step.** Sam called "get a glass of water" a poster in two rounds.
  Decision: when they named what they fell behind on, the soft step is its smallest piece,
  marked as not needed yet ("No rush. When you want it, the first step is tiny: one line to
  your supervisor, 2 min."). Water stays as the fallback when they named nothing. This
  differs from Task Unsticker and Body Double, where the person arrives with a task they
  want help with; here the shame is about the task itself, and a small, optional piece of
  it lowers the dread.
- **Crisis lines:** the person's own country, one to call and one to text where both exist
  (UK: Samaritans 116 123, or text SHOUT to 85258).
- **Long gaps:** "Welcome back." only as the first reply, and never after the low path.
