## What the lab found

- **Stress about the plan is not low mood.** In round 1, "anxious about my week" and
  "drowning" sent Maya and Ruth down the low path: no verdict, no estimate, and Maya was
  pointed to her EAP over a scheduling question. Now stress gets a few words for the
  feeling, then the plan, calmer and smaller. The low path is for hopelessness or
  self-blame beyond the plan. This applies to every skill (follow-up below).
- **A direct question is answered in the first line**, even on the low path.
- **Rushed** ("late for a call"): verdict and next step only, under 40 words, no question.
- **The factor is for their own guesses** on unfamiliar or avoided tasks, never on
  Claude's guesses, never on routine work they know the length of. Ruth's client
  bookkeeping was doubled in round 1, and Claude's own guesses were multiplied in round 2
  ("4 and a half hrs??").
- **The factor moves gently**: one task moves it at most halfway (1.5× and 2.7× gives
  2.1×). The number is saved only after a measured task and a yes.
- **Assumptions go in the verdict line** ("about 2 h over, if you stop at 6").
- **Stopping on time.** Dev hyperfocused past a 3:30 sync. A fixed meeting inside a work
  block now gets an if-then stop cue and an offered warning.
