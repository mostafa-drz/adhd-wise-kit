two weeks off (a holiday that turned into a slump) and only vaguely remembers three work threads.

## Persona's honest verdict

"The opening line promised a summary it couldn't give, and that nearly lost me. Then it made me
do the work, which I suppose was the point. But it found the one thing actually waiting on me in
under ten minutes, didn't make up a word, and never asked for my inbox, so I'd use it again,
which is more than I can say for most of these."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** with nothing connected, the long-gap opener "Here's the short version" introduces
  no version. Lee calls it out right away. The two rules clash when nothing is connected.
- **Turn 2:** it picks the vendor thread because someone might be waiting on it, and says that
  in one line. This is where Lee started to trust it.
- **Turn 4:** the draft uses blanks instead of invented terms, which is exactly what Lee asked
  for, and it says plainly that nothing was saved or sent.
