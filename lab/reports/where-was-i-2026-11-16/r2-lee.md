that was actually on fire is handled. Finance has the number too, so I'd open it again tomorrow."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** "Welcome back." stands alone with nothing connected, and the slump gets exactly one
  line. The round 1 complaint about "Here's the short version" doesn't come back. The slump line
  still reads slightly like a poster to a skeptic.
- **Turn 3:** right after the one sanctioned look, the Next step goes back to the work (open the
  terms, find the price line) instead of more digging. Since Lee wasn't sure who had the ball,
  the look didn't feel like homework.
- **Turn 4:** the buffer question comes before the draft. Lee rejects it with a reason, but it
  reads as a fair check rather than nagging, and the draft makes nothing up.
