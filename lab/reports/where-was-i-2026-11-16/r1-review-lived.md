## The one change

> Once they've told you what they were doing, the next step is doing its first slice, not more searching.

with:

> Once they've told you what they were doing, the next step is doing its first slice, not more searching, unless they don't know who has the ball; then one look of 3 minutes or less (newest subject line and date only) is the step.
