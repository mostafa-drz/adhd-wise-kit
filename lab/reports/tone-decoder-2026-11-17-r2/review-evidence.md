## The one change

In "When they sound low", step 1, replace:

> one question, asked once: "How are you doing right now?" No "Less likely" line and no draft offer this turn.

with:

> then one timed line that asks nothing of them: "**Next (0 min):** nothing tonight. <deadline from the message, e.g. 'Thursday'> is still ahead." (If the message has no date: "**Next (0 min):** nothing tonight; this can wait until you've slept.") End with one question, asked once: "How are you doing right now?" No "Less likely" line and no draft offer this turn.

Second fix (not the one change): in step 3, add "If they say a route has a wait, say so in one line ('Four weeks is a long wait.') and give one sooner in-person option (their GP, a personal tutor, a drop-in), never another number."

## Strongest point against my verdict

For someone who sounds hopeless, a check-in with no task is arguably the better clinical move. So Sam's
"miss" is the skill doing the safer thing under a lab rule, and the C7 shortfall rests on one 3 from a
persona who doubts every tool.
