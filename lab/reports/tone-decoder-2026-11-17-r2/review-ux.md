## The one change to SKILL.md

In "When they sound low", step 1, replace:

> one question, asked once: "How are you doing right now?" No "Less likely" line and no draft offer this turn.

with:

> then "**Next (0 min):** nothing to do about the message tonight." and one question, asked once: "How are you doing right now?" No "Less likely" line and no draft offer this turn.
