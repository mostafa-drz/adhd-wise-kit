## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** no plan, the list held safe, "eat something simple", and one question. This is the opposite of round 1, where Sam got a full plan.
- **Turn 2:** "I don't have anyone" got one real route (uni wellbeing) and UK-only lines, given once, at 57 words. When Sam said "not in danger" in turn 3, the skill gave no more numbers.
- **Turn 3:** the skill had no rule for a route turned down because of a wait rather than a place. It improvised by pointing to the GP booking already on Sam's list, and Sam took that up. It offered no task until Sam asked for one in turn 4.
