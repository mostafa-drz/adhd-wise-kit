## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** "assuming 1 h tonight" and the call parked rather than scheduled for the evening. Starting with "check the real renewal date" turned up a deadline a week sooner than she thought (turn 2).
- **Turn 2:** "Took 15 min against your 10" called the estimate hers when the skill had made it. She noticed and laughed it off in turn 3. The if-then cue ("morning tea") and the alarm time (9:15) pointed at two different moments.
- **Turn 2:** at 112 words, the second reply was the longest of the session. The three call sentences were what she valued most.
