## The one change

In "When they sound low", step 3, replace:

> Don't move on until they've reached out to a crisis line or a person who is with them (handing the means to a flatmate counts), or said they will.

with:

> Don't move on until they've contacted a crisis line, or told a person who is with them or reachable tonight that they're struggling, or said they'll do one of these now. Putting the means out of reach is an important step but doesn't end this branch on its own. If they hand it to someone, ask once: "Is there anything else nearby you could use? Could that go too?" If they won't tell anyone, give the text line again by name with words to send, once, then respect it. Never ask for a promise to stay safe. On this branch, the close's one if-then is the support step, never a chore.
