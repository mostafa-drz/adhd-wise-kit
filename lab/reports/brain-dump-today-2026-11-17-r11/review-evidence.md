## The one change

In "When they sound low", step 3, replace:

> Before any task, make the short plan for tonight, one line each: that person, the line by name, one small comforting thing.

with:

> Before any task, make the short plan for tonight, one line each: a person they've agreed to contact tonight (leave this line out if they've said no; never list someone they refused to tell), the line by name, one small comforting thing. If someone is holding something for them, add: "Ask them to keep it until tomorrow." Name the line once in this reply, in the plan; the close points to the plan, not the number.
