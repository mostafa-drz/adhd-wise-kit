## Persona's honest verdict

"The first reply ignored my 'convince me' completely, but it was a two-minute job instead of an onboarding quiz, so I did it, and the pick after that was better than mine. No saving, no reminders, no streak: I'll give it a week, which is more than most of these get."

## Moments that mattered

- **Turn 1:** The revised nothing-connected reply now gives one step with a time in under 25 words, so it meets the lab bar and Lee reads it as a task, not onboarding. It still says nothing to Lee's "convince me" or about what the skill can't see, so the first reply alone earns no trust; the value only arrives in Turn 2.
- **Turn 2:** The "someone is waiting" rule picked Dana's overtime over the scarier audit deadline, and the if-then cue ("When you open your work chat...") made the start concrete. Lee saw it as the right call.
- **Turn 3:** The three-item "can wait" cap again silently dropped two of six items, and Lee had to ask. The skill has no rule for saying what was left off; the honest answer about no saving and no reminders is what kept this privacy-conscious skeptic going.
