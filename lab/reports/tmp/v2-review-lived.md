## Verdict

**Fail.** Criterion 1 averages 3.3 (bar is 4), and two of three first replies miss the lab rule "one next step with a time, in under 60 words." Everything else clears the bar, and this is clearly better than v1: Ruth's phone script and Lee's Dana pick are the kind of help that would bring me back.

## The one change

In `SKILL.md`, replace:

> Rules for every reply: the one thing first, no more than three "can wait" items, no shame,
> plain words. **Never reply with only a question:** every first reply gives a step with a time.

with:

> Rules for every reply: no more than three "can wait" items, no shame, plain words.
> **Every first reply is under 60 words and opens with one step with a time, even on the low
> path.** A full first reply is only: `**Start (5 min):** <step>`, then `**Why this one:**
> <one sentence>`, then `**Can wait:** <up to three>`, then the offer. Save "Good enough" for
> the next reply. On the low path the step is for them, not the task: "**Next 2 min:** get a
> glass of water, then tell me how you're doing."

(The low path's step 1, "No task, no tips", then needs "No work task" so the two rules agree.)

## Strongest point against my verdict

Sam stayed because the first reply asked for nothing, so forcing even a 2-minute step on the low path could read as "fix yourself" to someone who feels hopeless, and the lab bar may be the thing that's wrong there.
