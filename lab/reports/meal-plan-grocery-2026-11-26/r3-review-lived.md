## SKILL.md changes, most important first

Each change below touches a tested path, so re-run the persona named before merging it.

1. **Shared lists: never edit the other person's items** (re-run Maya). Under "The weekly plan", step 6, and "The running list", step 1 (duplicates), add:
   > On a shared list, never change an item the person didn't add. If it covers what they need, skip theirs and say so: "Sam's peppers are on; you need 2, so I added 1 more." If names only partly match ("cheese" vs "cheddar"), ask once in a few words: "Is Sam's cheese the cheddar?"
2. **Low path: when they asked for a list up front, it never waits two turns** (re-run Sam). In "When they sound low":
   - Step 1, add: "If they asked for a list but named no items, end with: 'I'll make you a short no-cook list next.'"
   - Step 2, change the waitlist line to: "and let the list wait a turn, unless they asked for a list in their first message; then give the short no-cook list in this reply, after the route and the crisis line." Keep the 60-word limit by trimming the list to 5 items.
   - Swap eggs out of the example no-cook list: "bread, yoghurt, soup, fruit, something you like". Once they name something they like, replace "something you like" with it.
3. **Reminders permission: say the button they will see, on the yes** (re-run Ruth). In "Where the list lives", step 1, replace the permission sentence with:
   > On the yes, before making the list: "Your iPhone will ask about Reminders; tap Allow Full Access. It's how I add to your list."
   Check the exact iOS button text before shipping. Only add a claim about what Claude does or doesn't read if the tool really works that way.
4. **Add the "why use you" line** (asked for in all three rounds). Under "The running list":
   > If asked why not a notes app: "A notes app works if you open it. Here you say 'out of X' mid-thought, I skip repeats, and at the store it's sorted by aisle."
5. **Memory answer names only the option that fits.** In "Where the list lives", step 3, change the "will you remember?" script to end with the one option that fits where they are (iPhone: Reminders; laptop, Android or unknown: Todoist), as the Setup section already says.
6. **From-memory line goes above the store list.** In "At the store", add: "On a list read from memory, the From-memory line comes first; nothing else goes above the list."
7. **Weekly plan tidy-ups.**
   - Template block: replace "Protein:" with "Fridge:" and add "Frozen:" so it matches step 5.
   - Step 2: "A calendar event that is itself dinner (dinner out, party) means no meal that night; count it as 'out' in the header."
   - Safety: "Allergen notes only for allergies they named."
8. **Small closes** (optional). A "k" after the trip ended: "On the list for next time: <items>." When they say they might order online: "Paste it into a delivery app if that's easier." Never order.

## The one change that would help most

Change 1. A shared list is where trust is most fragile: one changed item can make someone stop letting the list near their partner's things. It is a two-sentence rule and Maya will show at once whether it works.

## Strongest point against my verdict

Fits this person is exactly 4.0, held up by four 4s and Sam's 3. One lower score from another reviewer would sink it. And Sam's 3 comes from a rule the skill follows on purpose, safety before the list. My answer: the safety lines all fired correctly and Sam said "honestly this was ok". The gap is when the list arrives, not whether Sam was kept safe. Change 2 moves the list one turn earlier without removing any safety line, and a Sam re-run will show whether it holds.
