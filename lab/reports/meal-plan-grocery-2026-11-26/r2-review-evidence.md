## Evidence for scores under 4

  where it is used, reliably). On the nothing-connected desktop path the list's only way into
  tomorrow is memory, and memory dropped olive oil overnight (turn 4). The skill has no rule
  for reading a list back from memory, and turn 3's "it usually carries them into a new chat"
  claims a memory behaviour the skill does not back.
- **7 = 3.** The one setup offer was spent in turn 3, so when memory actually lost an item

## SKILL.md changes, most important first

1. **Make "done" end the trip, not tick the exceptions** (Dev, criteria 6 and 7). In The
   running list step 3, replace the "Got it all", "done" sentence with:
   > "Got it all" or "everything except the cheese" ticks everything except what they name,
   > with no question: `Ticked 8. Cheese stays on.` Once an item is kept on this trip, a bare
   > "done" or "that's it" ends the shop and ticks nothing more: `Done. Cheese stays on.` Only
   > "got the cheese" ticks it. "Got it" with one item left ticks it; with more left, treat it
   > as "got it all" and say the count.
   And in step 4 (Staples), add:
   > If the staples line went unanswered, repeat it once, short, in the end-of-shop reply
   > ("Eggs and coffee still aren't on. Add them?"). After that, drop it until the next "what
   > do I need".

2. **Say where the list came from and what memory can do** (Lee, Ruth, criteria 6 and 7).
   In Where the list lives, option 3, add:
   > Reading the list back in a new chat from memory: show it, then one line: `From memory
   > (7 items). It can miss things. Anything to add?` If they say something is missing, that
   > reply may carry the setup offer once, even if it was offered in an earlier chat:
   > `Todoist keeps it on your phone and can't lose it (2 min).` If asked "will you remember?":
   > `I can't promise. I've asked memory to keep the item names; it can miss some. Copy the
   > list to be safe.` Never say memory "usually" or "will" keep it.
   Change "in one line after the list" to "as the last line of a reply". For option 1, change
   `Kept milk, coffee here.` to `Kept milk, coffee in this chat.` and give a reply for an empty
   list: `Nothing on your list yet. What do you need?`

3. **First reply time and plan load** (Maya, criteria 1 and 3). In Rules for every reply, add
   an example for a question: `One question (10 sec): anything in the fridge to use up?`, and
   say the step comes before any context. In The weekly plan step 5 and the template:
   > Show the meals, then the buy list as one line with the count: `To buy: 14 items (onion,
   > peppers, sausages, chickpeas, ...).` Show the grouped list only if they ask or nothing is
   > connected. Use the store groups (Produce, Fridge, Pantry, Frozen, Other) and drop empty
   > groups. When a meal makes leftovers, end its line with "makes Mon too" and repeat that
   > on the closing line.

4. **Let the low path follow the person** (Sam, criterion 4). In When they sound low, step 2,
   add:
   > If they did the food step, say so in three words or fewer ("Crackers count.") inside the
   > 60 words. If they turn a route down and also ask to move on ("can we just do the list"),
   > skip the retry and do what they asked, unless there is any hint of self-harm.
   In step 1, add: "If they named no items, say 'I'll make the list next.'" In The running
   list, add: "No items named: offer a short no-cook list of up to 7, then 'Remove any?'"

5. **Shape of meal calendar events** (Maya). In The weekly plan step 6: "On a yes, put each
   dinner's name on its evening as an all-day note, no time blocked, unless they ask for a
   time."

6. **Approved "why you instead of a notes app" line** (Lee, two rounds improvised): `A notes
   app is fine. I sort it by aisle at the store and turn "stuff for tacos" into a real list.`

7. **Siri, once, after a Reminders list is made** (Ruth, criterion 7): after `Made a
   Groceries list`, add one line: `Next time, you can also say "Hey Siri, add eggs to
   Groceries."` For the Allow pop-up, add a plain clause about scope only if the Reminders
   tool's real behaviour is confirmed; never claim it sees one list unless that is true.

8. **List on the phone, person on the computer** (Maya note 5, untested): "If their list is in
   Reminders but this chat has no Reminders tool, don't start a second list. Say: `Your
   Groceries list is in Reminders on your iPhone. Say 'add the plan' in the Claude iPhone app
   to put these on.`" Re-run Maya on the laptop to test it.

**Strongest point against this verdict:** both criteria miss by 0.2, and each rests on one
