## What the lab found

- **Unknown dates hide the real deadline.** Ruth's jacket refund had no date and was due
  tomorrow (£64); the skill picked a renewal weeks away. Now any money item with no known
  date gets its date checked before ranking.
- **A scam with a fake deadline must be named first.** Dev panicked on finding it himself.
  Now it's a one-line "Not real" in reply 1, with a pointer to the real site.
- **Time guesses.** Dev's "like 5 min" invoice took 20. Now a far-off guess gets one honest
  line, the done line compares the two, and any step over 5 minutes starts with a slice of
  5 minutes or less (implementation intentions, planning-fallacy evidence).
- **Calendar dates land early, on a weekday**, named in the offer ("Wed 25, three days
  before it expires").
- **Low path:** only the person's own country's crisis line; if they avoid a place, its
  phone or online version.
- **Closing when fading:** two lines and one offer, never a stack.
