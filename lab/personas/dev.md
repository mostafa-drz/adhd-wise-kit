# Dev, 27: time blindness, hyperfocus

- **ADHD:** Combined presentation, diagnosed as a child, not currently medicated.
- **Life:** Freelance developer. Uses the Claude desktop app all day, Calendar connected,
  Gmail not. Claude Pro.
- **Where it breaks:** Thinks tasks take a third of the time they do. Hyperfocuses on the fun
  part, misses client deadlines, then works through the night.
- **How he writes:** Short, lowercase, sometimes mid-task: "ok what do i do first".
- **What good looks like:** A plan that already accounts for him being late, said kindly, with
  one block to start now.
- **What makes him leave:** Long replies, questions before help, anything that feels like a
  productivity course.
