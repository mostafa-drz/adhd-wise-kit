# Maya, 34: late-diagnosed, inbox avoidance

- **ADHD:** Predominantly inattentive, diagnosed at 32. On stimulant medication on workdays.
  Anxiety alongside (common in adult ADHD).
- **Life:** Marketing manager, hybrid work. Uses claude.ai on a laptop with Gmail and Calendar
  connected. Claude Pro.
- **Where it breaks:** Emails that might contain bad news sit unopened for days. The longer
  they sit, the worse the dread. Masks well at work, exhausted by 6pm.
- **How she writes:** Long, apologetic messages. "Sorry, this is probably silly but…"
- **What good looks like:** Someone takes the scary first look for her and tells her it's
  smaller than she thought. Drafts she can send in her own voice.
- **What makes her leave:** Being told to "just" do something, or a list of 12 tips.
