# Admin Sweep

Admin isn't hard; it's invisible until it's late. This skill makes it visible: one list of
what's due, sorted by date and consequence, with the boring parts already prepared.

Rules for every reply: the most urgent item first, five items at most in "Do now", no
shame, plain words. **Never pay, submit, cancel, sign or send anything.** Prepare it, and
let the person do the final click.

## The flow

1. **Sweep.** With Gmail, Outlook, Drive or files connected, search the last 60 days and
   the next 60 days for:
   - bills, invoices, failed payments, overdue notices;
   - renewals and expiries: subscriptions, insurance, ID and passport, domains, licences;
   - forms and requests: "please complete", "action required", enrollment, tax documents;
   - appointments to book or confirm, and refunds or returns with a window.

   Read subject lines and the relevant lines only. A failed read is not "nothing due":
   say which source failed.
2. **Nothing connected?** Ask them to paste or list what's on their mind, or to connect
   Gmail or Drive in Customize → Connectors.
3. **Build the list.** For each item: what it is, the date, what happens if missed (a
   late fee, a lapse, a lost refund), and the amount when stated. Sort by date, then by
   consequence.
4. **Prepare.** For the top items, do the prep work now:
   - find the exact link, account page or form in the email;
   - pull out reference numbers, amounts and dates;
   - draft any email that's needed (asking for an extension, booking an appointment);
   - turn a form into a short checklist of what they need to gather.
5. **Name the human part.** For each item, one line: "Only you can: log in and press Pay
   ($84.20)." This is the 2-minute version of the task.

## What the reply looks like

```
**Do first (2 min):** <the human part of the most urgent item>

**Do now** (due within 7 days):
1. <item>, due <date>: <consequence>. Ready: <what's prepared>. You: <the human part>
2. ...

**Coming up** (8–60 days): <item, date>; <item, date>

**Handled or not needed:** <count>, e.g. auto-paid bills

Want me to draft the <email> or put these dates on your calendar?
```

## Connectors

Offer to add due dates to the calendar or tasks to a task app, and only do it after a
yes. With memory available, save one line per recurring item ("Car insurance renews every
March") after they agree, so next year's sweep catches it early. Never save amounts,
account numbers or IDs.

## Privacy and safety

- Never show full account numbers, passwords, card numbers or ID numbers; mask them.
- Flag anything that looks like phishing ("your account will be closed, click here")
  instead of preparing it.
- Not financial, legal or medical advice. For anything with legal or tax risk, suggest
  checking with a professional.
