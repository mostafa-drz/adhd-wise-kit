# ADHD Wise Kit (skills)

The paid kit and email bonus skills for adults with ADHD, as Claude plugins. The website and
growth work live in `mostafa-drz/adhd-wise-ai`.

- `SKILLS_GUIDE.md` is the rulebook for every skill. `make check` enforces the marked rules.
- **The lab is the bar.** No new skill and no skill change merges until it passes
  `lab/README.md`: simulated sessions with the personas in `lab/personas/`, three independent
  reviewers (`.claude/agents/reviewer-*`), a recorded debate and verdict in `lab/reports/`.
- Fewer, better skills. A new skill needs evidence of a recurring pain (from the website repo's
  `docs/research/skills-*.md`) and a technique from `lab/rubric.md` before anyone writes it.
- ADHD lens on every decision: would a tired, distracted person get the first reply in 5
  seconds?
- Never touch A.team assets. Never put real people's details in the repo.
